RefillAll gases
STEP 1 · AIR

It starts with the air we breathe.

Air is about 21% oxygen. It is separated by cryogenic distillation: air is cooled until its gases turn to liquid and part ways.

STEP 2 · LMO TANK

Stored at −183 °C.

Liquid medical oxygen sits in our own cryogenic tank in Ratlam. One litre of liquid becomes roughly 860 litres of oxygen gas.

STEP 3 · FILLING

Filled, tested, sealed.

Every cylinder is checked, filled to the right pressure and tested for leaks before it leaves the plant.

STEP 4 · DELIVERY

At your door, on time.

Hospitals, nursing homes, workshops and factories across the region get steady supply, with full and empty cylinders tracked.

Questions

Good to know.

Do you refill our own cylinders?

Yes. We refill party-owned (PC) cylinders at our plant after checking their condition and test date.

Do you supply medical oxygen to hospitals?

Yes. We manufacture medical oxygen in our own LMO plant and supply cylinders and liquid oxygen to hospitals, clinics and nursing homes.

Can you deliver?

Yes. We deliver in and around Ratlam. Call or WhatsApp with your location and quantity to confirm timing.

Are refrigerant cylinders returnable?

Yes. R22, R134a and other refrigerants come in returnable cylinders. Return the empty cylinder when you take the next one.

How do I store gas cylinders safely?

Keep cylinders upright and chained, in a cool, airy place away from flames, oil and grease. Close the valve when not in use, and never roll or drop a cylinder.
